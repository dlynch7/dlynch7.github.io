This capstone course was held in-person for the Winter 2020 quarter and switched to remote for the Spring 2020 quarter due to the COVID-19 pandemic.\
Instructors: Prof. Ed Colgate and Billie Strong\
Responsibilities:
* I personally developed a MATLAB-based simulation framework that enabled the course to pivot from from designing, building, and controlling real robots to simulating them, necessitated by the COVID-19 pandemic.
* I taught guest lectures on robot kinematics, dynamics, and nonlinear control
* I guided students on how troubleshoot mechanical designs, electrical circuits, embedded C code running on ARM microcontrollers, and physics-based simulations in MATLAB
