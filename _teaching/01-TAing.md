## Robot Design Studio (ME 495) - Winter-Spring 2020 and 2019
Robot Design Studio (RDS) is a capstone course in both mechanical and electrical engineering at Northwestern University.
It is also open to early graduate (master's and doctoral) students.
I enrolled in the course in 2018, the first year it was offered, and I enjoyed it so much that I came back as a TA the next two years.

RDS was held in-person for the Winter 2020 quarter and switched to remote for the Spring 2020 quarter due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
That year, I personally developed a MATLAB-based simulation framework that enabled the course to pivot from from designing, building, and controlling real robots to modeling and simulating them (and controlling them in simulation).

Instructors: Prof. Ed Colgate and Billie Strong (2020), Dr. Ron Worth (2019)\
Responsibilities:
* Taught guest lectures on robot kinematics, dynamics, and nonlinear control
* Guided students on how troubleshoot mechanical designs, electrical circuits, embedded C code running on ARM microcontrollers, and physics-based simulations in MATLAB
* Grading

## Scientific and Embedded Programming in Python (ME 224) - Spring 2021 and 2020, Fall 2020 and 2019
Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, this course was entirely virtual when I TA'd in 2021 and 2020.
The pivot to a virtual classroom in 2020 was a new experience for the whole teaching team (Prof. Peshkin, myself, and my fellow TAs) as well as the students, and we collectively undertook the additional responsibility of supporting the students and maintaining some semblance of normalcy during that challenging time.

When I TA'd in Fall 2019, I co-ran (with another TA) a 4-hr lab on CircuitPython that met 3x/week.

Instructor: Prof. Michael Peshkin (Spring 2021 and 2020) and Prof. Malcolm MacIver (Fall 2020 and 2019)\
Responsibilities:
* Teaching guest lectures on Python programming and guiding students on how to troubleshoot code
* Holding weekly office hours over Zoom
* Grading
