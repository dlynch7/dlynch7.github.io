I delivered an oral presentation of (very) preliminary results on research into generalizing the spring-loaded inverted pendulum (SLIP) template to yielding terrain.
These preliminary results showed that the dynamics of the coupled robot-terrain system can be quite rich (i.e., complex!) and deserving of closer study, which led to another [talk](2023APSMM) at the following year's APS March Meeting and, eventually, culminated in a [journal paper](../publication/lynch2024efficient), published in IEEE Transaction in Robotics.
