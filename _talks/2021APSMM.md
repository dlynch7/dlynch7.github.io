I delivered an online oral presentation on research into planning period-one hopping gaits on deformable (i.e., yielding) terrain, using resistive force theory to model the physics of planar foot-ground interaction and using direct collocation trajectory optimization to generate feasible gaits.
You can watch a [video](https://youtu.be/yvgUBHpQkeA) of this presentation on YouTube.
This work taught me about the potential and limitations of trajectory optimization.
I am using it as a starting point for some of the methods going into another [journal paper](../publication/lynch20xxplanar) on planning and control for dynamic locomotion on complex terrain via reduced-order/simplified models, currently in preparation.
