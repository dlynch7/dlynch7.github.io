I delivered an oral presentation of preliminary results on dynamic planar hopping on yielding terrain using a reduced-order model of foot-ground interaction and tools from nonlinear dynamics.
I am developing these preliminary results into a [journal paper](../publication/lynch20xxplanar), currently in preparation.
