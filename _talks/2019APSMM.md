I delivered an oral presentation of preliminary results on the so-called "soft landing problem" of how to minimize energy loss from ground deformation when a legged robot impact yielding terrain (e.g., granular media).
These preliminary results led to a [journal paper](../publication/lynch2020soft), published the following year in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.
