I delivered an oral presentation of preliminary results on the nonlinear dynamics of a simple spring-legged monopedal robot hopping on deformable (i.e., yielding) terrain.
These preliminary results led to a [journal paper](../publication/lynch2024efficient), published late the following year in IEEE Transaction in Robotics.
