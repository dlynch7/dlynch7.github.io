Education
======
* Ph.D in Mechanical Engineering, Northwestern University, 2026
* B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, University of Notre Dame, 2016

Research experience
======
* 2022-2026: Journeyman Fellow (DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory (ARL) and Northwestern University)
  * Collaborated with ARL researchers to advance the state of the art in soft-soil legged robotic locomotion while simultaneously pursuing doctoral research.
  * Delivered guest lectures on mechanics of robot-soil interaction to Autonomous Systems Branch and presented research to Technical Advisory Board (TAB) review meeting at ARL Aberdeen Proving Ground.
  * Fueled collaborative relationship between ARL and Northwestern University's Center for Robotics and Biosystems.
  * Supervisor: Jason L. Pusey

* 2016-2026: Graduate Researcher (Northwestern University - Center for Robotics and Biosystems)
  * Researched dynamic legged locomotion on yielding terrain, combining reduced-order modeling of legged robots and dissipative yielding substrates, systematic experiments with fully custom robot hardware, and theoretical and computational tools from nonlinear dynamics and optimal control.
  * Published two first-author papers in top-tier robotics journals (IEEE Transactions on Robotics and IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters).
  * Delivered oral presentations and poster presentations at ICRA (2020 and 2025) and the Global Physics Symposium (formerly the March Meeting) of the American Physical Society (2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2025).
  * Collaborated with and mentored five undergraduate students and two master's students.
  * Supervisors: Prof. Kevin M. Lynch and Prof. Paul B. Umbanhowar

* 2014-2016: Undergraduate Researcher (University of Notre Dame - Locomotion and Biomechanics Laboratory)
  * Investigated inertial effects on egocentric and allocentric proprioception and motor control using a cobot system designed for diagnosing and rehabilitating motion-planning disorders.
  * Supervisor: Prof. James Schmiedeler

* Summer 2015: Undergraduate Researcher (University of Notre Dame - International Summer Undergraduate Research Experience)
  * Collaborated with visiting Tsinghua University undergraduate engineering students on feasibility study of direct-drive valve actuation methods for Honeywell's Advanced Technology division, culminating in on-site presentation at Honeywell to engineering R&D team.
  * Supervisor: Prof. Bill Goodwine
